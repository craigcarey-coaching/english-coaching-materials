The Brief

Bring these back today

Your upgrades from last time. Try to use two of them while you talk.

    1

    Take it in

    Take as long as you need. There is no rush.

    2

    First take

    Answer out loud. Think first, then speak. Silence is fine.

    3

    How the argument works

    Every piece is trying to persuade someone of something. Take it apart. Say your answer first, then make a note.

    4

    Write your version

    Choose one format. Notes are enough; you will speak from them, not read them.

    Stretch one sentence

    Choose a sentence that feels safe or ordinary. Say it aloud in two different ways, then write the version you like best.

    5

    Disagree well

    First, respond to the piece in your own words: where do you agree, and where not? Then open the toolkit.

    Have you given your own response out loud?

    Tap the phrases you want to use in your brief. Neutral works anywhere. Formal suits meetings and writing. Direct is firm; use it with care.

    6

    Deliver it

    Say it twice: first short, then complete. Don't read your notes word for word.

    The corridor version Done

    You meet your manager in the corridor. "I hear you looked into this. What's the headline?"

    Give the main point and one reason. Nothing more.
