Past tenses: make it automatic

You've seen the rules. Now practise until you don't have to think about them. Do the steps in order. Each one has a target: when you reach it, move on.

About 50 minutes in total. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

1Rules check

Read the six rules once. Then answer twelve questions. Before each answer, say which rule you're using.

Rule 1 · Finished timeA finished time → simple past.
yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2020, when…?
Rule 2 · did + baseDid / didn't + base verb. Never two past forms.
Be uses was / were, not did.
Rule 3 · In progressBackground action → was / were + -ing. The interruption → simple past. Know, want, need, like: no -ing.
Rule 4 · Connected to nowExperience, unfinished time, a result now → have + participle.
ever, never, already, yet, this year, so far
Rule 5 · The depuis trapPast until now with for / since → present perfect. Never the present.
Rule 6 · The third columnAfter have / has, use the participle: written, taken, seen, been. Not wrote, took, saw.
