4 · Read · 12 min

A right-to-disconnect policy

Lumen Group: Right to Disconnect Policy (extract)

1. Purpose

At Lumen Group, we believe that rest is essential for health and for good work. This policy explains how we protect employees' time outside working hours.

2. Normal working hours

Normal working hours are 8:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.

3. Rules for everyone

Employees don't have to read or answer work emails, calls or messages outside normal working hours. Managers mustn't send messages after 19:00 or at weekends, except in an emergency. When possible, staff should use the "schedule send" function. Employees must not be criticised for not replying outside working hours.

4. Exceptions

Staff who are on call must answer urgent calls during their on-call period.

5. Raising a concern

If you feel pressure to work outside your hours, you should speak to your manager first. If the problem continues, you can contact the HR Wellbeing Team or a staff representative.

6. Review

HR must review this policy every year with staff representatives.

A. True or false?

B. Find every word in sections 3–6 that talks about obligation. Which one means it's forbidden? Which means it's not necessary?

Then choose the right form.

C. Your verdict as an arbitrator

An employee tells you: "My manager broke this policy." What does the policy not tell you?

  • What is an "emergency"? Who decides?
  • What happens if a manager breaks the rules?
  • What about personal phones and WhatsApp?
  • What would you add?
